SomaRelease

for a body that carries you

With SomaRelease, I want to bring the signals of your body to the fore, in order to find and look at the emotional connections stored there.

The mind has a great influence on our body – on how we go through life, bent and heavy or upright and light. Every cell of our body stores everything we experience. Unfortunately, not everything is well integrated, and that can then unconsciously cause problems such as pain or tension.

Together we find out which emotions are stored in the tissue and work on the structures – for better body awareness, more flexibility and improved performance.

“So that you are connected on all levels and feel at home in your body again”

ThetaHealing®

when change is allowed to go deeper

What we believe about ourselves influences how we think, feel and act.

Many of our limiting patterns and beliefs arise from the context of our lives: the experiences of past family generations, the culture and the environment we come from.

We are often not aware of them, yet they work deep within us and influence how we think, feel and act.

ThetaHealing® helps to become aware of these deeply rooted beliefs and patterns and to change them.

for freer thinking and acting
for more openness and possibilities
