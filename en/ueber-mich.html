About me

My name is Susanne Kreutz Lamm, and my work as a physiotherapist has taught me that change happens where we recognise blocking and burdensome behaviour, understand systems and processes, and want to stand up for a better “today”.

Through my work as a health and life coach, I want to build a bridge between working with the body and working with the mind.

In coaching, we look, feel and think precisely where emotions cause physical symptoms, or where physical symptoms give rise to negative emotions.

To do this, I combine various techniques such as NLP, wingwave®, SomaRelease, ThetaHealing® and much more, so that we can integrate your experiences together.

My journey

  1. 1969

    Born in Saarbrücken, where I also went to school.

  2. since 1992

    Qualified physiotherapist. Since then I have regularly attended further training in pain therapy, fascia therapy and holistic work.

  3. 1998 · 1999

    Mum of two children who have grown into wonderful people and of whom I am very proud.

  4. 2003 – 2006

    Training as a craniosacral therapist at the Institut für Integrative CranioSacral Therapie in Switzerland. This training changed my entire way of thinking and working. It was here that I first came into contact with NLP techniques and experienced what the mind and the way we communicate can do to ourselves and our body.

  5. 2012 · 2014

    Training as NLP Practitioner (2012) and NLP Master (2014).

  6. ThetaHealing®

    My need to understand and connect things kept growing deeper, and I wanted to accompany people ever more comprehensively. This is how I came across ThetaHealing® and attended its courses over several years: Basic, Advanced, Dig Deeper and Game of Life. This training enables me to accompany people through deep processes as well and to release old patterns that reach right into the present.

  7. 2026

    Certified wingwave® coaching method – for me the missing piece of the puzzle to build a bridge between psyche and soma and reconnect the two.
